# ShopCo Customer Policy

ShopCo is a fictional UK online retailer. All prices are in GBP (£).

## 1. Delivery

1.1 Standard delivery takes 3–5 working days from the order date.

1.2 An order is "delayed" if it has not been delivered within 7 working days of the order date.

1.3 For a delayed order, the customer may choose either a full refund or to keep waiting. We will also offer a £5 goodwill voucher.

1.4 If an order is delayed by more than 14 working days, it is treated as lost and the customer is entitled to a full refund.

## 2. Returns

2.1 Customers may return most items within 30 days of the delivery date for any reason.

2.2 Items must be unused and in their original packaging, unless they are faulty.

2.3 Return postage is free for faulty items. For change-of-mind returns, the customer pays return postage.

2.4 Orders that are still "processing" can be cancelled for a full refund without a return.

2.5 For change-of-mind returns, the customer must send the item back first. The refund is issued only after we receive the returned item, so no refund is processed when the customer first asks.

## 3. Refunds

3.1 Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 working days.

3.2 Refunds over £50 need approval from a human team member before they are issued. The agent must not promise these refunds; it should tell the customer their request is being reviewed.

3.3 Refunds of £50 or less may be approved automatically if the order is eligible under sections 1 or 2.

3.4 An order that has already been refunded or returned cannot be refunded again.

## 4. Faulty or Damaged Items

4.1 If an item arrives faulty or damaged, the customer must report it within 14 days of delivery.

4.2 The customer may choose a replacement or a full refund. Refunds still follow section 3.

## 5. Communication

5.1 We reply to all customer messages within 1 working day.

5.2 We never ask customers for passwords or full card numbers.

5.3 Replies should be polite and clear, and should state which policy section applies.
